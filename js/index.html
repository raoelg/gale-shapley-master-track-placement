Waiting list

Numbers under college names give indication of chance (%) that a student will be accepted in the college after the expected number of placed students have decided to withdraw.

Disclaimer: These probabilities are estimated from a limited set of simulations and hence may vary from one use of this program to the next. The simulations make a very simplistic assumption that student withdrawal is uniform across Tracks and independent from their initial placement. Simulations take some time; please be patient.
